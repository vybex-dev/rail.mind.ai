About Project

Hackathon-built railway intelligence for FAR AWAY 2026

RailMind AI is a unified railway intelligence platform built for the FAR AWAY 2026 hackathon. It brings together three specialised AI modules — delay prediction using XGBoost trained on 50,000 journeys, real-time crowd forecasting for 5 major stations, and CLIP-powered track defect classification — into one cohesive product. The goal: give Indian Railways operations teams the fastest, sharpest decision-making layer possible.
